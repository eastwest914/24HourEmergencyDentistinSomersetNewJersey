Frequently Asked Questions

Should I go to the ER or an emergency dentist?

For tooth pain, a broken tooth or a dental infection, an emergency dentist can treat the cause directly. For trouble breathing or swallowing, or a serious head injury, go to a hospital.


Do you offer same-day appointments?

Yes. We are open 24/7, so call 732-447-9606 and we will arrange the soonest visit possible.


What can I do for tooth pain until I arrive?

Rinse with warm water, gently floss around the tooth and use a cold compress on the cheek. Avoid placing aspirin directly on the gum, and call us for guidance.


How do you handle payment?

We accept cash and credit cards, and we go over the cost of your care before treatment starts.

Emergency Root Canal

Lingering pain, a swollen gum or a tooth that hurts when you bite can mean infection inside the tooth. An emergency root canal clears out the infected pulp, relieves pressure and protects the tooth from further damage.

Saving your natural tooth keeps your bite stable and avoids the need for a replacement. We use modern methods so the visit is comfortable and efficient.

Tooth Extraction and Wisdom Teeth

If a tooth cannot be saved, or a wisdom tooth is impacted and painful, an extraction may be the best way to end the problem. We numb the area thoroughly and take our time so you feel as little as possible.

After the procedure we review healing, diet and pain control, and we are available if you have questions overnight. We can also discuss replacement options when you are ready.
